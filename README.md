# Kota Bengkulu Profile Website — Bumi Rafflesia

An informative static website introducing **Kota Bengkulu** (*"Sekundang Setungguan Seiyo Sekato"*), highlighting the history, geographic profile, popular tourist destinations, and traditional cuisine of Sumatra's west coast. Built as an early front-end web development project and published via GitHub Pages.

**Live Demo:** [https://rahmahf074.github.io/Website_Bengkulu_R_2025/](https://rahmahf074.github.io/Website_Bengkulu_R_2025/)

---

## Key Sections & Content

- **General City Overview:** Administrative facts (anniversary, area size, population, area/postal codes), regional languages & ethnic groups (Melayu Bengkulu, Rejang, Serawai), tropical climate details, and transportation access.
- **History of Bengkulu:** Origins of the name *Bengkulu* (*Bangkai di Hulu*, *Bencoolen*, *Bangkulon*), local kingdoms, British and Dutch colonial periods, and its establishment as the capital of Bengkulu Province.
- **Geographic Profile:** Astronomical and geographical location along the west coast of Sumatra between the Indian Ocean and the Bukit Barisan mountain range, including climate patterns and the natural habitat of *Rafflesia arnoldii*.
- **Popular Tourist Destinations:**
  1. Fort Marlborough (*Benteng Marlborough*)
  2. Bung Karno's Exile House (*Rumah Pengasingan Bung Karno*)
  3. Baitul Izzah Grand Mosque (*Masjid Raya Baitul Izzah*)
  4. Gedung Merah Putih
  5. View Tower
  6. Bengkulu State Museum (*Museum Negeri Bengkulu*)
- **Traditional Bengkulu Cuisine:**
  1. Pendap
  2. Lempuk Durian
  3. Kue Tat (Bay Tat)
  4. Bagar Hiu
  5. Tempoyak Durian
